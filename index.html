About Our Bakery

Cherry Blossom Bakery started in 2021 as a small corner shop in Warje, Pune. Our family loves baking classic pastries mixed with gentle floral and fruit flavors. Everything on our shelves is mixed by hand and baked fresh each morning—no artificial flavors, just flour, butter, eggs, and care.
